## Part 5: The LinkedIn Algorithm Playbook

**To maximize reach on each article:**

1. **Post a short teaser 2 days before** — "Article dropping Thursday. Here's the one thing I didn't expect when I started researching it." Builds anticipation.

2. **Publish articles on Tuesday or Wednesday morning** — peak enterprise LinkedIn browsing time.

3. **First comment is yours** — Comment on your own article within 1 hour of posting with the single most important takeaway. This seeds engagement.

4. **Tag 3–5 people in your network** who are relevant to the topic — not to promote, but to genuinely ask "curious what you think about #2." Only tag people you actually know.

5. **Respond to every comment in the first 48 hours** — each reply extends the post's reach. Treat it like a live conversation.

6. **Repost your articles as short-form posts** 6 weeks later — "I wrote this a month ago. It's the most-read thing I've published. Here's why I think it landed:" + link. Gets second-wave traffic.
